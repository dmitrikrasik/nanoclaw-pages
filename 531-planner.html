5/3/1 Planner

Wendler 5/3/1 percentages applied to any training max — barbell lift, weighted pull-up, weighted dip, whatever. For weighted lifts enter a TM in kg (bodyweight exercises with added load: TM = added weight only, e.g. vest/belt). For calisthenics with no added weight, choose Bodyweight (reps) and the percentages are applied to your max reps instead.

New wave

Bump every training max by a fixed amount (typical: +2.5kg upper body, +5kg lower body; +1 rep for bodyweight exercises) to start the next cycle.

Standard 3-week wave + deload: Week 1 = 65/75/85% × 5/5/5+, Week 2 = 70/80/90% × 3/3/3+, Week 3 = 75/85/95% × 5/3/1+, Week 4 (deload) = 40/50/60% × 5/5/5. The "+" sets are AMRAP (as many reps as possible) — the number shown is the minimum target, go past it. If you don't know your true 1-rep max, enter a rep max instead (e.g. "5 reps at 20kg added") — it's converted to an estimated 1RM via the Epley formula, then the training max is taken as a percentage of that (90% is the Wendler-standard default, adjustable). For Bodyweight (reps) exercises the same percentages are applied to your training max in reps (max strict reps × the percentage you pick, 90% by default) and each set is rounded to whole reps, so a 12-rep max gives a 10.8-rep training max and week 1 reads 7 / 8 / 9+ reps. No weight is involved at all. Data is saved locally in this browser only.
